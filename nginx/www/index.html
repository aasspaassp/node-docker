About

After working in education and marketing, I discovered programming in 2021. Currently completing a computer science degree at UTEL while building projects that merge technical skills with my love for electronic music.

Most recent professional experience was as a Junior SRE at Boletia, resolving technical issues related to Go, AWS, and PostgreSQL databases.
